### More to read

These optional projects show other ways to generate a view or simulate a drive. Their use inside Tesla’s v15 visualizer remains unverified.

- **DashSplat** is the closest visual comparison for the talk. Ryan Vogel used six cameras from his own Model 3 to recreate a chase view. The README shows how calibration, Gaussian splatting, vehicle tracking, and image refinement contribute to the result, and where the pipeline struggles. A similar-looking output alone cannot establish which architecture Tesla used. [Author’s repository](https://github.com/R44VC0RP/dashsplat)
- **StreetCrafter** uses LiDAR-derived geometry to guide video diffusion. Look at how the geometry constrains what appears when the virtual camera moves. [Project and paper](https://zju3dv.github.io/street_crafter/)
- **Generative Camera Dolly** takes one video and a target camera pose, then generates the same scene from the requested perspective. Its demos make viewpoint conditioning easy to explain. [Project and paper](https://gcd.cs.columbia.edu/)
- **DrivingForward** reconstructs a 3D Gaussian scene from vehicle-mounted cameras. Trace how the images become a representation that can be rendered from another viewpoint. [Authors’ code and paper](https://github.com/fangzhou2000/DrivingForward)
- **Instant NuRec** reconstructs a recorded multi-camera driving scene in one forward pass. Its benchmark measures the time to reconstruct a clip. Measuring display freshness would also require timestamps for observation and playback. [NVIDIA paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14203)
- **DriveDreamer4D** generates additional views with a driving world model to improve reconstruction of a scene as it changes over time. Look at how the method handles moving vehicles. [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Zhao_DriveDreamer4D_World_Models_Are_Effective_Data_Machines_for_4D_Driving_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
- **OmniDreams** targets closed-loop driving simulation. Read how prior frames, simulator state, and the vehicle’s next action condition the next camera observations. [NVIDIA research overview](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/projects/omnidreams-blog/index.html)
- **Cosmos-Dreams** is NVIDIA’s 2B-parameter driving simulation model. Its description explains how map and trajectory inputs steer the generation of multi-camera video chunks. [NVIDIA model description](https://developer.nvidia.com/drive/downloads)

## Architecture reading guide

Start with **Figure 2** of Tesla’s 2024 patent application. Pick one 3D grid cell beside the car. Follow its query into the features extracted from the camera images, then follow the resulting 3D representation as it combines with earlier time steps. Figures 3A–3B pair camera views with an example occupancy output. Explain what the model predicts about that cell before discussing how another model could generate a display image. The filing describes one historical design. On the v15 slide, draw only the connections described for that release. [Tesla filing, searchable text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240185445A1/en) · [Original illustrated PDF](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/26/14/1d/568b7bb36c61cb/US20240185445A1.pdf)

For a Tesla-hosted account of a shipped release, read the **AI & Software** section of Tesla’s Q1 2026 update. Tesla identifies three changes in v14.3 involving the vision encoder, reinforcement learning during training, and the compiler/runtime. Its reported figure of up to 20% lower inference latency refers to v14.3. Musk’s proposed 35% reduction concerns the v15 visualizer. [Tesla Q1 2026 update](https://assets-ir.tesla.com/tesla-contents/IR/TSLA-Q1-2026-Update.pdf?pubDate=20260422)

For optional background beyond the scheduled TeaCache reading, use the caching references to ask **what gets reused, across which steps, and what breaks when motion changes**. Trace one method alongside Tesla’s account of reusing a generated view. For profiling, distinguish operator time from queueing and transfers. NVIDIA tooling describes how to measure a proxy workload. Tesla’s TRIP operator placements, numerical precision, and SRAM traffic for v15 would require Tesla-specific evidence.
